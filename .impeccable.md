# Rate Matcha — Design Context

## Target Audience
Local matcha enthusiasts in Toronto. People who care about the quality and craft of matcha, not just casual coffee-shop visitors.

## Use Cases
1. Discover new matcha spots in Toronto
2. Read community reviews before visiting
3. Write and share their own reviews

## Brand Personality & Tone
**Cozy & artisanal, clean & modern.** The design language of a well-curated specialty tea shop — warm, considered, unhurried. Not corporate, not cutesy. Think a high-quality food magazine meets a thoughtful neighbourhood guide.

## Design System (implemented 2026-03)

### Aesthetic Direction
"The Matcha Gazette" — editorial/artisanal. The hallmark is café names rendered in Cormorant Garamond (a high-contrast serif), giving every listing an editorial quality. The hero uses large italic Cormorant with "Matcha" in the brand green — a magazine masthead moment.

### Fonts
- **Display**: Cormorant Garamond (headings, café names, brand wordmark, stat values)
- **UI**: Plus Jakarta Sans (body, labels, buttons, metadata)

### Color Palette (OKLCH)
All neutrals are warm-tinted toward hue 85 (paper/parchment feel):
- `--cream`: `oklch(96.2% 0.009 85)` — page background
- `--surface`: `oklch(99% 0.005 85)` — cards, nav, modals
- `--matcha`: `oklch(44% 0.09 148)` — muted earthy green (like real matcha powder)
- `--matcha-dark`: `oklch(33% 0.085 148)` — deep matcha, used for CTAs
- `--amber`: `oklch(71% 0.155 65)` — stars and warmth accents
- `--ink`: `oklch(17% 0.012 85)` — warm near-black text

### Key Design Decisions
- Hero: cream background (NOT a dark gradient), left-aligned large serif, soft radial glow decoration
- Sort/filter: clean pills without surrounding box, no emoji
- Cards: taller image (195px), café name in display serif
- Nav: minimal, wordmark only (no emoji), underline tab indicator
